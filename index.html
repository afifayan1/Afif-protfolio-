About

Who I am

I'm Afif Ayan, a 16-year-old aspiring aerospace and electrical engineer. I started this journey at age seven or eight, taking apart and building small circuits just to see how things worked.

Since then I've grown into rocketry, aeronautics, and avionics — using Fusion 360 to design airframes and parts, KiCad to design the electronics and flight circuits, and C++ to write the code that controls them. Robotics is still part of the toolkit, but rockets and flight systems are where I'm focused now.

This page is where I share what I'm building right now and the projects I've finished along the way.
